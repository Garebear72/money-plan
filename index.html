Get started

Load your plan to begin

Money Plan works from one plan file: your paycheck, bills, rent, debts and so on, written in JSON. Load it once and everything is saved only on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. The app makes no network requests at all.

  1. Choose your plan file below, or open it in another app, copy all of it, and paste it into the box.
  2. Tap Validate to check it. You'll see a summary of what will be loaded.
  3. Tap Import. You can change everything later in Settings.

Don't have a plan file yet? Look at the example plan for the format. It contains made-up numbers.

Import purchases from your bank

Add many purchases at once from your bank's activity file (CSV). The file is read on this device and never uploaded.

  1. On chase.com in a web browser (the Chase app can't export), open your checking account and tap the download icon above your transactions. If the site keeps sending you to the app, turn on Desktop site in the browser menu.
  2. Pick a date range, choose CSV, and download.
  3. Choose that file below. Bills, transfers and purchases you've already logged are left unticked.
